[ 01 ] WHAT THIS IS

A day festival for everyone dreaming of the Grand Line and raw collective sound.

LAUGH TALE isn't an awkward convention floor. It's a five-hour explosive anime gathering packed with high-octane DJ performances, live musical acts, curated merchandise booths, gaming zones, and cosplay energy that doesn't hold back.

Doors open at high noon, lights drop, and the room moves as one. No corporate fluff, no awkward seating—just sound, lights, anime fever, and people who share the exact same obsession.

Bring the crew you actually like. That's the whole guest policy.

[ 04 ] HOUSE RULES

Two rules.
The first one is the party.

UNTOLD creates a space where community, freedom, and music collide. Everything else is negotiable—the outfit, the energy, the songs you scream along to.

!

Respect the Cosplay & Community.

Cosplay is not consent. Ask before clicking pictures, respect fellow crewmates, and leave the gatekeeping outside. Any disruptive behaviour or harassment results in immediate ejection with zero refund.
